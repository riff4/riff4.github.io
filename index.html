About AirTourer

Overview

AirTourer is a location-based tourist audio guide mobile application designed to explore cultural and historical heritage in complete autonomy. The app was built with passion to deliver a seamless, enriching experience that strictly respects travelers' privacy—without the constraints of tour groups, fixed schedules, or user accounts.

Key Features & Highlights

Interactive Map & Free Roam

Live distance calculations in volatile device RAM and secure, ephemeral spatial queries to Supabase PostGIS to explore monuments around you freely.

Themed Guided Routes

Follow immersive walking tours of 7 to 15 stops with step-by-step guidance, automated proximity detection, and interactive route trails to visit an entire city at your own pace.

Exploration Badges & Gamification

Unlock cultural milestones and listening rewards as you explore (50, 88, 300 POIs discovered). Your progress is stored locally in SecureStore.

Background Audio Guides

Listen to narrations even when your screen is locked or the application is minimized, powered by the dedicated FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK foreground service.

Free Credits & Rewarded Ads

100% free model: 1 credit = 1 unlocked point of interest. Replenish your balance for free (5 free credits) by watching a short Google AdMob rewarded video ad.

Privacy & Zero Account

No account, no email address, and no password required. Your data (credits, unlocked POIs, badges, language) is encrypted locally on your phone via SecureStore.

2. Traitement des Données de Géolocalisation & Requêtes Serveur (Traitement éphémère)

Engagements stricts et transparence sur la géolocalisation :

  • Traitement local en temps réel (RAM de l'appareil) : Le suivi continu de votre position (affichage du marqueur sur la carte, calculs de distance pour le guidage pas-à-pas et les parcours guidés, boussole d'orientation) s'exécute exclusivement dans la mémoire vive (RAM) de votre smartphone.

Cette transmission a pour unique finalité d'interroger l'index spatial pour renvoyer la liste des monuments et audioguides correspondants.
  • Aucune conservation / Aucun historique / Suppression immédiate : Ces coordonnées ne sont JAMAIS enregistrées sur le serveur, ni journalisées dans des fichiers de logs, ni associées à un compte utilisateur (aucun compte n'existe dans l'application), ni cédées ou revendues à des tiers. Dès que la requête spatiale est exécutée et le résultat renvoyé, les coordonnées sont immédiatement purgées de la mémoire serveur. Ce traitement répond pleinement à la définition d'un traitement « éphémère » (ephemeral processing) au sens de la fiche de sécurité des données Google Play (Data Safety).
  • Aucune localisation en arrière-plan : L'application n'utilise pas et ne demande pas la permission d'accès à la position en arrière-plan (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION). Dès que l'application est minimisée ou fermée, toute lecture du signal GPS s'arrête immédiatement.
  • Contrôle et liberté de l'utilisateur : Vous pouvez autoriser ou refuser l'accès à la position GPS lors du lancement de l'application ou à tout moment dans les réglages de votre smartphone. 2. Geolocation Data Processing & Ephemeral Server Requests

Strict commitments and transparency regarding geolocation:

  • Real-Time Local Processing (Device RAM): Continuous tracking of your position (displaying your location pin on the map, real-time distance calculations for step-by-step guidance and thematic routes, heading compass) is performed exclusively in your smartphone's random access memory (RAM).
  • Ephemeral Search Queries (HTTPS / TLS): When you search for points of interest (POIs) nearby or explore a thematic circuit, the coordinates of the center of the viewed area and the search radius are transmitted ephemerally via a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS / TLS) to our hosted database (Supabase / PostgreSQL PostGIS). This transmission's sole purpose is to query the spatial index to return the corresponding list of monuments and audio guides.
  • Zero Storage / Zero History / Immediate Purge: These coordinates are NEVER saved on the server, never logged in access logs, never linked to any user account (no user accounts exist in the application), and never sold or shared with third parties. As soon as the spatial query completes and results are returned, coordinates are immediately purged from server RAM. This processing strictly qualifies as Ephemeral Processing under the Google Play Data Safety specifications.
  • No Background Location Tracking: The application does not request and does not use background location permissions (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION). As soon as the application is minimized or closed, all GPS polling stops immediately.
  • User Control & Choice: You can grant or deny location access upon opening the app or at any time in your smartphone's system settings. Denying location permissions does not prevent manual browsing of POI details, circuits, or audio guides.

3. Zero-Account Model & Secure Local Storage (SecureStore)

To maximize user privacy, AirTourer adheres strictly to data minimization (Privacy by Design):

  • Zero Sign-up / No Account Required: No account creation, email address, user name, or password is required to use the application.
  • Local Encrypted Storage (SecureStore): The only application state data necessary for functionality is stored locally on your smartphone via an encrypted and secure storage mechanism (SecureStore). This includes:
    • Your free listening credits / token balance;
    • The list of unlocked point of interest IDs;
    • Exploration badges and milestones achieved during your visits;
    • Interface and audio guide language preferences (French / English).
  • This data remains strictly stored on your physical device and is never transmitted to remote servers.
